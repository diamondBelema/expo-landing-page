Big update — v11.1.1

Here's what changed.

We reworked Claudy from the ground up. This is an early experimental rollout — things might be rough in places. Your feedback directly shapes what we fix next.

Workspaces — organize your way
Group your subjects and personal vaults into custom Workspaces (think folders). Your Home screen now shows daily progress and a "Review Due" lane so you always know what needs your attention first.
Simpler navigation
5 tabs down to 4 — Home (Workspaces, progress, review), Practice (games, spaced repetition, AI tutor), Discover (full library, public vaults), Insight (mastery heatmap, milestones, credits).
Vault Marketplace
Discover public vaults made by other users. Vote, comment, and preview content before unlocking with credits. Made a vault you're proud of? Publish it to the marketplace.
Unified subject hub
Tapping into any subject or vault takes you to a single hub with tabs for Concepts, Resources, and Practice — no more hunting across screens.
Fresh Material 3 design
We've adopted Google's Material 3 Expressive design system with distinct shapes for different content types (curriculum, vaults, games, library) and smoother motion throughout.
Retired game modes
To make room, we've retired Survival Mode, Daily Challenge, Elimination Gauntlet, Spell It Out, and Memory Flip. Spaced repetition and core practice tools remain.

The problem

Students fail not because they're lazy.
They fail because no one told them what to read.

The Nigerian university experience is brutal. Here's why students struggle.

01
Too much material, no filter
You download everything. You read nothing useful. By the time exams come, you've covered 20% of what matters and 80% of what doesn't.
02
Cramming instead of learning
Last-minute panic cramming doesn't stick. You pass, forget everything, and start over next semester with zero foundation.
03
No idea what lecturers actually test
Every lecturer has a style. Some love definitions, some love application. Without knowing the pattern, you're shooting in the dark.
The fix

Claudy gives you
the edge.

  • Vault Marketplace
    Discover public vaults made by other users. Vote, comment, preview before unlocking. Made a vault? Publish it to the marketplace.
  • AI that predicts what comes out
    Our engine analyses past questions and course patterns to surface the topics most likely to appear in your next exam.

Features

Your entire study system,
in one app.

Workspaces — organize your way
Group subjects and personal vaults into custom Workspaces. Your Home screen shows daily progress and a "Review Due" lane so you always know what needs attention.
4-tab navigation
Home · Practice · Discover · Insight. Each tab has a clear purpose. No more hunting across five screens.
Vault Marketplace
Discover public vaults, vote and comment, preview content before unlocking. Publish your own vaults too.
Curated materials
Notes, summaries, and past questions — handpicked and organized by course. No more hunting through random PDFs.
Unified subject hub
Every subject and vault has a single hub with tabs for Concepts, Resources, and Practice — all in one place.
Expert flashcards
Manually crafted for maximum exam relevance. Every card covers a concept that actually shows up in PAMO exams.
Quiz system
Practice with real-style questions. Test your understanding before the lecturer does.
Practice games & spaced repetition
All in the Practice tab — Match Pairs, Blitz Mode, Definition Snap, plus the AI tutor and spaced repetition scheduling. Study doesn't have to feel like studying.
Offline downloads
Download any subject to your device and study without data or Wi-Fi.
Leaderboard & XP
Earn XP for every session. Climb the leaderboard against other students.
Daily streak
Log in every day to build your streak. Miss a day and it resets.
Spaced repetition mastery map
A visual map of every topic shows what you've mastered, what needs review, and what you haven't touched.
AI tutor & explanations
Stuck on a concept? Tap the AI button on any card or question for a clear explanation tailored to your level.
Profile & milestones
Your mastery heatmap, achievements, and credits — all in the Insight tab.
Progress tracking
See your weak areas. Know what you've covered. Never waste a study session again.
Material 3 design
Fresh shapes, purposeful motion, and a distinct look for every content type.

The maker

Built by a student,
for students.

Hi — I'm Diamond. I'm 17, and I've been writing code since I was 12. Programming was never just a hobby for me — it was how I learned to solve real problems.

When I got to PAMO and saw how students struggled — drowning in slides, not knowing what to study, cramming the night before — I couldn't just look away. I knew I could build something.

It started as EduHive — an offline AI study app that generated flashcards and quizzes directly on your device. It worked, but the on-device AI was too slow for real-world use. So I rethought everything and built Claudy: faster, sharper, school-specific.

I have a big dream for this project. I'm going to fulfil it — one update at a time.
