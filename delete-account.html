Account management

Delete your Claudy account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. This action is irreversible — please read through before proceeding.

This cannot be undone
Once you submit this request, your account and all personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. You will lose access to all your study progress, saved content, and flashcard history. This action cannot be reversed.

What gets deleted

Your account & login credentials — email, password hash, and session tokens
Study progress — flashcard history, quiz scores, and course completion data
Personal preferences — selected university, level, and notification settings
Waitlist & contact records — your email from our waitlist database
What we may retain
Anonymised analytics — aggregate study pattern data with no link to your identity
Legal records — transaction or support records if legally required, for a limited period

Before you go — consider these

Turn off notifications
If notifications are the issue, you can disable them in app settings or your device settings without losing your data.
Request a data export
Want to keep your study history but leave? Email us and we'll export your data before deletion.
Send us feedback
Have a complaint or issue? Tell us at diamondbelema78@gmail.com — we'll try to fix it.
Take a break
Your account and progress will be here when you come back. No need to delete — just close the app.

How deletion works

Submit this form
Fill in your registered email and confirm your intent. You'll receive a confirmation email within a few hours.
We verify your request
We match your email to an active account and may send a verification link to confirm it's really you.
Data deleted within 30 days
All personal data linked to your account is permanently erased from our systems within 30 days of your request.
You receive a final confirmation
We send a final email confirming that deletion is complete. That's it — you're gone from our records.
